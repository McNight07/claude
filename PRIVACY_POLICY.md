## What information we collect

**Account information.** When you create an account, we collect the name,
email address, and password you provide. Your password is never stored in
plain text.

**Content you create in the App.** Your quiz answers and results, favorited
careers, bookmarked certifications, certification and roadmap progress,
study log entries, and profile details (experience level, interests,
preferred fields, and an optional profile photo).

**Device permissions.** If you choose to set a profile photo, the App
requests access to your photo library. We only access the specific photo you
select.

## How your information is stored

stores this information, and we do not sell or share it with third parties.

personal information is sent as part of it.
